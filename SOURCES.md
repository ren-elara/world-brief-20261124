# Reporting for 24 November 2026

24 November edition. Each page explicitly dates its report 24 November 2026.
Illustrations and headline wording are original; no source photographs or other
artwork are copied. Numbers describe the reporting at retrieval, not final totals.

1. **Middle East shipping.** Al Jazeera's closed live page identifies its updates

   the tankers claim to Iran and does not suggest who laid the mines or that the
   port has already been attacked following the order.

2. **Huawei verdict.** BBC News, Rachel Clun: a Brooklyn jury found Huawei guilty
   of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud, among 10 guilty counts,
   but acquitted it of racketeering conspiracy. Both parts of the verdict appear
   in the image. The illustration uses a generic server and gavel, not a logo.
   https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmy49ngydqzpo

3. **Russia–Ukraine energy attacks.** Al Jazeera with Reuters: Putin threatened
   retaliation on Tuesday after Ukrainian attacks on Russian refineries. The

   https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/putin-says-kyiv-hit-russian-oil-plants-despite-trump-plea-vows-retaliation

4. **DR Congo withdrawal.** Al Jazeera with Anadolu: the Convention for the
   Popular Revolution announced Tuesday that it had halted its advance towards
   Bunia and withdrawn from recently seized areas after fighting with government
   forces. This is CRP, not M23, and does not signify a nationwide peace agreement.
   https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/rebel-group-retreats-in-dr-congo-after-heavy-clashes-with-government-forces

5. **Cartagena hostages.** Al Jazeera with Reuters: the Colombian president said
   Tuesday that 30 people had been taken hostage and five released, leaving about
   25 held at a supermarket after an attempted armoured-vehicle robbery. The image
   attributes the reported count and makes no claim about the eventual outcome.
   https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/hostage-situation-reported-in-colombia-as-armed-men-flee-into-supermarket

6. **Xiamen aircraft order.** Reuters via Al Jazeera: a Tuesday stock-exchange
   filing says China Southern subsidiary Xiamen Airlines signed an agreement
   to buy 50 Boeing 737-8 aircraft, subject to shareholder and government
   approvals, with delivery in 2031–2034. The image describes a deal, not delivered
   planes, and omits the inconsistent list prices in the page's body and caption.
   https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/24/china-southern-airlines-unit-to-buy-50-boeing-737-8-planes
